FELLOWS OF THE SNAKE — notes and trivia, with spoilers

These notes contain spoilers. Read the novel first.

  • Almost all action happens in Manhattan Beach, California, at Mira Costa High School.
  • When Barbara, in the brief discussion with Len after crash-the-party, said that "some authors ignored even the horrors of their own time," she meant L. Frank Baum — and "ignore" was the wrong word to use. Probably Nina would know more.
  • Mentioned in "The Origin" and relevant to Takeover: the Vulnerable World Hypothesis.
  • SAPIEN = Self-Amplifying Post-Intelligent Evolving Network. MAGI = Meta-Amplified Generated Instance.
  • As a clean break after Takeover, the wizard-assigned names of the polities differ from those of the dismissed political entities, but are usually close to them, and taken from human history or culture. For example, Calida, in Southern California. Or Erewhon, for a polity in New Zealand. Huzan is a historic Chuvash word for Kazan. Other examples: Suzdal and Zichia.
  • One of the competition teams is named Parsimus, a Finnish word for an item to be darned — a bold ambition for a team aiming at not only diagnostics but repair. Similar motivation must have been behind Eir.
  • Mike (in chapter 1) must have chosen the Jacquard machine for his essay because of its importance for computing.
  • The novel contains exactly one frown and no raised eyebrows at all. The author hopes this does not disqualify it from the SFF genre.

Whether this becomes the first book in a series depends partly on how it is received. If you enjoyed it, a review on Amazon helps other readers find it.
